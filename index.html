Financial Calculation Transparency & Methodology

Net Profit / Loss Formula: (Net Sale Proceeds + Received Dividends) - Total Invested Capital

Buy Fee Calculation: Gross Purchase Price × settings.buyFeeRate (1.12%)

Annualized Return (XIRR): Solved using Newton-Raphson root finding over individual dated cash flows: Σ [ CF_i / (1 + r)^((t_i - t_0)/365) ] = 0.

Break-Even Price: The exact market price needed such that: (BreakEvenPrice × Shares) × (1 - SellFeeRate) + Dividends = Total Invested Capital.
